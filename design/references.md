# Visual references

What the diagrams and the site are drawn from. Kept as links and descriptions, not copied images: the work belongs to other people and this repo is public.

## The diagrams

**[Rare Earth Hypothesis](https://www.behance.net/gallery/85211131/-Rare-Earth-Hypothesis)**, a pair of bilingual information design posters on Behance: *Rare Earth Hypothesis* in ultramarine, and its companion *Selenogony* (theories of the moon's origin) in red-orange. The red one is the direct ancestor of the CIVICWORKS ink. Together they are the source of the drawing language in `prototypes/`.

What is being borrowed:

- **One ink on white.** A single saturated colour does all the work: ultramarine on one poster, red-orange on the other. The posters sit on light grey; here the paper is pure white (`#ffffff`), as in the current navigation and place prototypes, and the ink is `#d63c23`. Not off-white, not grey.
- **Solid ink, a pale tint, and nothing between.** Bodies are flat pale tints of the ink (salmon on the red poster, lavender on the blue), outlined in full-strength ink; the one thing that matters in a vignette (an impactor, a moon) is the only solid fill.
- **A second colour only to mark contrast.** Where the blue poster summarises the competing moon theories it switches to the red, so colour means "the other case", never decoration.
- **Texture instead of colour for fills.** Diagonal hatching, cross-hatching and stipple at fine weights, plus flat light tints of the one ink. A legend is a row of texture swatches.
- **Three line weights.** Hairline for structure and leaders, thin for outlines, one heavier stroke for frames and emphasis. (The navigation prototype's 0.5 / 0.75 / 1.25 comes from this.)
- **The circle as the unit.** Small circular vignettes, each showing one step or one condition, often in a row joined by thin arrows.
- **Labels on leader lines,** small and light, with a short paragraph of explanation sitting beside each drawing rather than under it.
- **Headings marked by a short vertical bar,** a larger title over a smaller subtitle, light sans serif throughout.
- **Poster grid.** Columns split by dashed rules, the whole sheet framed by one heavier border. Each poster sits over a large hatched circle, the motif at sheet scale.
- **Cutaways and sequences.** Wedge sections of a planet with each layer in a different texture; numbered process steps (1 to 5) read left to right; small particle scatters for debris, accretion and dust.
- **Charts in the same hand:** dot matrices instead of bars, scatter on a fine grid, bars filled with texture.
