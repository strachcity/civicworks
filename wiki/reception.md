# Reception

Substack performance, 13 pieces, Sept 2025 to Jul 2026. Pulled 2026-08-20.

**Read this file with suspicion.** It says which arguments travelled, not which are right. Reach on Substack is mostly decided by things outside the text: whether someone with an audience shared it, whether the network recommended it, what was in the news that week. With n=13, everything below is suggestive at best.

The reason it exists is narrower: `mechanisms.md` identified a spine by reading the archive. This is an independent check on whether readers found the same thing. They did.

## Signpost pieces acquire nothing

**05** (a pointer to the Recurve article) and **08** (an event write-up) converted **zero subscribers between them** from 494 views. They are the only two posts in the archive that did.

Both are the shortest pieces, both are the least argued, and both point somewhere else for the substance. 08 in particular is a strong argument with no room to make it: the fintech conditionality material is real and gets about 900 words.

If a piece is worth publishing here, it needs the argument in it.

## What the open rate is not telling you

Open rate falls almost monotonically: 74, 62, 66, 67, 53, 59, 55, 46, 45, 49, 49, 45, 42.

This is list growth, not decline. Early subscribers are self-selected and highly engaged; every new cohort dilutes the percentage while absolute reach rises. Reach over the same period went from 318 to 3,180. The checker also finds piece 13 the cleanest in the archive by a wide margin.

Do not read the open rate as a quality signal.

**Same trap with likes per view.** It is *highest* on the lowest-reach posts (05 at 26.4, 02 at 20.7, 08 at 18.7). Low-reach posts are read almost entirely by the committed core; high-reach posts pull in passers-by who read and leave. Likes/view measures audience concentration, not quality.

## Two things to watch, not act on

**Practice notes are inconsistent.** 07 (citizens) converts at 29.4 per 1,000, the best of any real piece. 12 (test and learn) converts at 10.0, the worst. Same format, three times apart. Possibly subject: 07 is about participation and portable anywhere, 12 is about a specific programme. One pair proves nothing.

**07 provoked the most discussion per reader** (4 comments on 680 views). But comment counts across the archive run 0 to 5, which is noise, not signal.
