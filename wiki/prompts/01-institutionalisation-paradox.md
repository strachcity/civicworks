# The institutionalisation paradox

**Status:** ready. The strongest thing the corpus has and no one has written it.

## The claim

Protected spaces for learning face a bind, not a design problem. Without institutionalisation their practices stay peripheral. With it, they lose the freedom that made them work. Twenty years of labs, studios, units and NewCos are the same experiment run repeatedly, and the pattern is not that the model was wrong but that no one has resolved the bind.

## Why it can be written now

Four independent arrivals at the same formulation, from four different evidence bases, none of which can see the other three:

- **Thesis** (Sept 25), from 15 interviews across 4 countries: "without it, practices risk remaining peripheral; with it, they may lose the freedom and adaptability that makes them effective in the first place."
- **09** (Mar 26), from the studio model: how a studio gains authority rather than informing from a distance, "still working through".
- **11** (May 26), from institutional history: whether a NewCo holds protected authority as it scales, or is absorbed by the governance logic that constrained MindLab, Helsinki Design Lab and GDS.
- **12** (Jun 26), from inside a live case: "Do they get absorbed back into it? Or do they diffuse into nothing once the attention moves elsewhere? We are too early to know."

The fourth is his own, current, at CustomerFirst. Almost no one else can write the theory and the live case together.

## Evidence in hand

The lab history in 11 (MindLab folded, Helsinki Design Lab closed in 2013 after SITRA concluded embedding beat concentrating, GDS institutionalised). The thesis fieldwork on intermediaries compensating for weak capabilities and staying precarious. The DVLA "NewCo" language travelling in 12, which he reads as either weight or capture and cannot yet tell apart.

## What is missing

A resolution, or an honest account of why there is not one. The nearest thing in the corpus is a bet on sequencing: 11 on early GDS, where authority, learning and delivery stayed connected "long enough for evidence to accumulate", and 09's "legitimacy in this space is built through action, not insight." Untested in both.

Also missing: any case of a protected space that did successfully institutionalise without flattening. If one exists, it is the piece. If none does, that is the piece.

## What would make it fail

Writing it as a survey of labs. The history is the setup, not the subject.

Resolving the paradox too neatly. The strength here is that four separate investigations hit the same wall; a tidy answer at the end would betray that and would not be believed.

Leaving CustomerFirst out to stay safe. 12 already published the uncertainty, and the honesty is the piece's authority. Section 1 of the voice file: own practice gets the same rigour as any other case study.
