# Samples

Finished, published pieces. Ground truth.

`voice-dna.md` describes where the voice has arrived. These are the writing it describes, and when the two disagree, the writing wins.

## What goes here

Finished work only. Published essays, practice notes, GDS blog posts, papers, and the occasional email or LinkedIn post worth keeping. Not drafts, not fragments, not AI output.

One file per piece, named `YYYY-MM-DD-slug.md`, with a short header:

```markdown
---
title: 
published: YYYY-MM-DD
format: essay | practice-note | gds-blog | paper | short
url: 
---
```

Recent work outweighs older work, so the dates matter.

## Why they earn their place

Two uses.

Reading a new draft against real pieces catches things no rule catches: whether the paragraphs build the way his do, whether the ending has the quieter, shorter register the file describes.

And adding a piece here is the moment to read it against `voice-dna.md` and log the disagreements. Most editing happens outside this repo, in Substack or a text editor, and none of it is visible to the learning loop. A published piece dropped in afterwards carries more signal than a week of in-session edits.
