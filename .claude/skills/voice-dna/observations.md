# Observations

Evidence from real sessions. Candidates, not rules.

Nothing here is a rule until it has been promoted into `voice-dna.md` and recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`. See `references/learning.md` for how capture, promotion and pruning work.

Newest first.

Entries arrive one of two ways. `scripts/observe.py diff <draft> <final> --append`
generates them from a real edit, and they need their Context lines filled in and
their duplicates merged. Or they get written by hand when Jack corrects
something mid-session.

`scripts/observe.py status` shows what is ready to promote.

**Status:** `active` (apply now, overrides `voice-dna.md` on conflict) · `watching` (hypothesis, do not act on it) · `promoted` · `retired` (kept on purpose: a wrong guess is worth knowing).

**Tags:** `argument` `structure` `rhythm` `register` `vocabulary` `punctuation` `formatting` `format-specific` `openings` `endings`.
