# Prototypes

Interactive companions to published pieces, served on GitHub Pages. They are artifacts made from the writing, not part of the wiki, and nothing in `wiki/` or `archive/` should point here as a source.

Only this folder is published, by `.github/workflows/pages.yml`, on any push to `main` that touches it.

## What is here

**[navigation/](navigation/)** goes with *Tasting the organisational treacle*. A 3D sketch on one screen. The cone lies on its side, narrow citizen end on the left, and drifts on its own when left alone. Drag it any way, trackball style, and it turns all the way round in that direction; flick it and it tumbles, slows, then brings the nearest of six faces round (value, legitimacy, capacity and the edges between them). A note points at whatever is facing you. A dotted trail records where the experiment has settled, and each thing it turns up swells that layer and stretches that corner of the triangle, which keeps its straight edges, so the drawing ends up a different shape from the one it started as. Layer names sit under their rings, and a pace scale sits just below the drawing like a ruler, reading from an afternoon to a parliament or more; it holds still while the model turns, and both fade end on. End on, the three questions show under their corners. `og.png` is the share image. One self-contained HTML file, no libraries. Earlier versions are kept as snapshots, [v0.1.0](navigation/v0.1.0/), [v0.2.0](navigation/v0.2.0/), [v0.3.0](navigation/v0.3.0/), [v0.4.0](navigation/v0.4.0/) and [v0.5.0](navigation/v0.5.0/).

**[navigation/materials/](navigation/materials/)** is the next step for the navigation model, kept at its own address so the original stays as it is. It adds the design materials of institutional change (legislation, contracts, review cycles, professional roles and the rest) as a Materials mode inside the same model, not a fourth axis. Pick a material and it lies across the rings it reaches, on the side of the corners it pulls on, with the materials it moves with as faint dotted arcs; pick a second to compare. The note says what it is, where it reaches, its pace, a question an experiment might ask, and, once probed, what an experiment might turn up and what changing it would need. Encountering, learning and changing stay separate: selecting is encountering, probing is learning and grows depth, and nothing on the page changes a material. With materials on, the pace ruler gains a second track for how fast a decision can be taken, which is not how fast the arrangement underneath changes. A walk-through follows a fictional service where people show they're still eligible. The materials, pair notes and the walk-through live in `materials.js`, apart from the drawing code. `capture.js` cuts an essay still from the live page, `materials-depth.png`, 1456 px wide: the model stripped back to its rings, with legislation and policy and the materials it moves with as points on the layers they reach, joined by one dotted loop, and only the materials named. Materials v0.1.0, built on navigation v0.6.1.

**[place/](place/)** goes with the same essay's "Experimentation as architecture" section, and is built like the navigation prototype: one page that draws the stack in code from a projection, in the same two tones, line weights and type, so it can turn later without a redraw. v0.3.0 follows the current draft. The layers are social infrastructure, governance, operational infrastructure and culture over the place of work, and each is drawn with what it's made of instead of a plain texture: people and the relationships between them, a route through gates, a ring of modules, and slow strata. It opens on the organisation alone and switches to side by side with the thesis neighbourhood (after Pagh & Cook 2023), where physical infrastructure becomes operational infrastructure, nature becomes culture and the neighbourhood scale becomes the place of work. The copy sits at the top of the page's script, so labels are easy to swap. **[place/planes/](place/planes/)** is an alternative after an exploded isometric drawing: the same layers as separate planes, pulled apart and staggered, each with a small line-drawn scene, labelled alternately left and right. `capture.js` exports the essay images: `place-diagram-single`, `place-diagram`, `place-diagram-mobile` and `planes/place-planes`, each as a 1456 px PNG and an editable SVG. `alt.txt` holds the alt text. [v0.2.0](place/v0.2.0/) and [v0.1.0](place/v0.1.0/) keep the earlier versions.

**[probes/](probes/)** is a working page comparing seven directions for one small editorial illustration in the treacle essay's "Experimentation as probes" movement: A, a woven cloth of materials with one thread pulled; B, a hanging mobile rebalanced by a small weight, with legislation on a fixed joint; C, a probe's path through a stippled field that thickens, going round neat rings and through tangles; D, an experiment reaching beneath the surface of a service and lifting one thread of the materials stitched together underneath; E, materials as pieces in rows, two swapped in two rows so the gaps line up into a route; F, a probe pushed down through sticky layers until legislation holds. G, after a sketch, the whole system as a circle whose top edge is the visible service, with a spotlight shining across it from an experiment to the far side, the materials and the fine lines between them coming into view in the light one relationship at a time, other connections showing only where they cross the light, and the rest hatched as the service's dark matter, where everything stays hidden. On the page the light can be widened, narrowed and aimed, bringing other materials out of the dark and letting others go back into it. Each has a control to play with and a note on what it shows and what it loses, and the page ends with a comparison. The drawings come from `illustrations.js`, and `capture.js` exports each as a 1456 x 640 PNG and a standalone SVG for the essay. A also comes as two stills that show the movement without a slider, one with arrows in the gaps of the cloth for which way each part moved, and one before and after with the arrows on the second. None of them is in the essay yet.

## Rules

- A prototype goes with the essay and doesn't argue more than the essay does. If it needs its own explanation, it has become a framework product.
- Examples come from published text only, or are generic. Nothing from internal sources.
- No scoring, ratings or "where is your organisation?" diagnostics.
- No tracking.
