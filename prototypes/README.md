# Prototypes

Interactive companions to published pieces, served on GitHub Pages. They are artifacts made from the writing, not part of the wiki, and nothing in `wiki/` or `archive/` should point here as a source.

Only this folder is published, by `.github/workflows/pages.yml`, on any push to `main` that touches it.

## What is here

**[navigation/](navigation/)** goes with *Tasting the organisational treacle*. A 3D sketch on one screen. The cone lies on its side, narrow citizen end on the left, and drifts on its own when nobody is touching it. Drag up and down to spin it onto one of six faces (value, legitimacy, capacity and the edges between them), left and right to look end on (across) or from the citizen end (through). A note points at whatever is facing you, and depth builds up as you go. One self-contained HTML file, no libraries. Earlier versions are kept as snapshots, [navigation/v0.1.0/](navigation/v0.1.0/) and [navigation/v0.2.0/](navigation/v0.2.0/).

## Rules

- A prototype goes with the essay and doesn't argue more than the essay does. If it needs its own explanation, it has become a framework product.
- Examples come from published text only, or are generic. Nothing from internal sources.
- No scoring, ratings or "where is your organisation?" diagnostics.
- No tracking.
