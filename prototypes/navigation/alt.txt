navigation-1-model.png
A cone lying on its side, narrow at the left where citizen interaction sits and widening through four more ringed layers, service processes and technology, organisational priorities, cross-institutional arrangements, and political and strategic support. Inside it, a triangle reaches out to three corners, value, legitimacy and capacity. One ring, cross-institutional arrangements, is drawn heavier, and a dotted trail runs back through the layers to show where an experiment has been.

navigation-2-end-on.png
The same model seen end on. The layers collapse into rings inside a triangle, and each corner carries a question. Value asks whether people will use what we've made and what value it might create. Legitimacy asks whether we have the support and permission to pursue it. Capacity asks whether the organisation can actually deliver it. The triangle is pulled out of shape where learning has stretched its corners.

navigation-3-side-on.png
The cone seen side on, with each of its five layers labelled from citizen interaction to political and strategic support. Below it, a pace ruler runs from an afternoon through months, a budget year and years to a parliament or more, getting slower to the right. Years is marked, matching the cross-institutional arrangements layer, which is highlighted.
