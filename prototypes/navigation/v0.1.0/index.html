The model has three dimensions, drawn as one object. Across are three questions that never line up neatly. Value asks whether people will use what we've made and what value it might create. Legitimacy asks whether we have the support and permission to pursue it. Capacity asks whether the organisation can actually deliver it.

Through are five layers of an institution, from the smallest part upwards. Citizen interaction, service processes and technology, organisational priorities, cross-institutional arrangements, and political and strategic support. They are drawn as rings on a cone that widens and fades as it rises, because you rarely know which layers matter until you hit them.

Depth is the learning that comes from moving across and through. It is drawn as a sphere that grows as the experiment reveals dependencies, starts new conversations and challenges assumptions.

Focus the drawing to turn it with the keyboard. Left and right arrows turn it to face the next question. Up and down arrows move between layers. Page up looks from above, page down looks from below, and home looks from the side. The buttons and the layer list do the same things.
