Experimentation as navigation

An experiment moves across value, legitimacy and capacity, and through the layers of an institution. What it learns can reshape both.

The model has three dimensions. Across are three questions that never line up neatly. Value asks whether people will use what we've made and what value it might create. Legitimacy asks whether we have the support and permission to pursue it. Capacity asks whether the organisation can actually deliver it.

Through are five layers of an institution, from the smallest part upwards. Citizen interaction, service processes and technology, organisational priorities, cross-institutional arrangements, and political and strategic support. They are drawn as a cone that widens and fades as it rises, because you rarely know which layers matter until you hit them.

Depth is the learning that comes from moving across and through. It grows as the probe reveals dependencies, starts new conversations and challenges assumptions.

The probe is a slider. Use up and down arrows to move through layers, left and right arrows to move across the three questions, or the buttons below the drawing.
