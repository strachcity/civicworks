An organisation drawn as five stacked layers, after the neighbourhood layers in the author's thesis. From the top, social infrastructure, governance, physical infrastructure and culture sit above the place of work at the base, where each of these comes together and becomes lived experience for the people doing the work.

Side by side, a neighbourhood drawn with the same five layers sits on the left. Nature becomes culture, and the neighbourhood scale becomes the place of work.
