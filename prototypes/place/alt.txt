place-diagram-single.png
An organisation drawn as five stacked layers, labelled on the left. Social infrastructure is drawn as people joined by lines, governance as a route through gates, operational infrastructure as a ring of modules, and culture as slow strata. They sit above the place of work, where these arrangements meet and where we can look for what an experiment leaves behind.

place-diagram.png
Two stacked diagrams side by side, a neighbourhood on the left and an organisation on the right, with dotted arrows joining matching layers. Social infrastructure and governance stay the same. Physical infrastructure becomes operational infrastructure, nature becomes culture, and the neighbourhood scale becomes the place of work.

The same two diagrams stacked for a phone, the neighbourhood above and the organisation below, joined by a dotted arrow.

planes/place-planes.png
Five planes pulled apart and staggered, each with a small scene. Social infrastructure shows two groups of people with dotted lines joining them across the groups. Operational infrastructure shows a grid of modules, a few of them built on. Governance shows a route through three gates, one of them open. Culture shows a long-standing building and people of more than one generation on slow strata. At the base, the place of work shows desks and people, where these arrangements meet.
